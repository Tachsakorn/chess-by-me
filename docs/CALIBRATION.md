# Rating estimate calibration

The rating sections are generated by `scripts/calibrate-rating.script.ts` (`npm run script -- calibrate-rating`) on
2026-11-13T14:02:11.211Z; every number in them was written by that run. The last section,
[Accuracy against Lichess and Chess.com](#accuracy-against-lichess-and-chesscom), was added by hand
on 2026-11-15 from scratch runs. The script rewrites this whole file, so copy that section back
after running it again.

## Data

- Source: `https://database.lichess.org/standard/lichess_db_standard_rated_2026-10.pgn.zst` (Lichess open database, CC0), month 2026-10.
- Streamed and decompressed on the fly; nothing downloaded was saved. The request was stopped
  after 64.7 MB of compressed data.
- Games read: 198380. Games kept: 20000.
- Skipped: no-eval 177884, short 218, time-control 210, bot 45.
- A game is kept when it is standard chess from the start position, has both ratings, a
  `base+increment` time control, at least 20 plies, and a `[%eval]` on every move (a
  checkmating last move has no `[%eval]` in the file and is read as a finished game).
- Time class follows Lichess: `base + 40 * increment` seconds; under 180 bullet (UltraBullet,
  under 30, counts as bullet), under 480 blitz, under 1500 rapid, otherwise classical.
- Each game gives two rows (one per player): the player's Lichess rating, our accuracy for that
  player (`computeAccuracy` on the file's evaluations, start position at +15 cp), the time class
  and the player's number of moves.

## Method

- A random 20 % of games (seed 20261113; both players of a game on the same side) is held out.
- From the other 80 %, per time class and for `all`: accuracy buckets 5 points wide; the 20th
  and 80th percentile of rating in each (linear interpolation). Under-filled buckets (fewer than
  200 rows) are merged, smallest first, into their smaller neighbour.
- The measured percentiles do not rise perfectly with accuracy (noise between neighbouring
  buckets, and a dip in the 95-100 bucket). Each end of the range is therefore replaced by the
  closest sequence that never falls as accuracy rises (pool-adjacent-violators, weighted by rows;
  `src/analysis/rating/monotone.ts`). The tables below show both the values used and the
  measured ones.
- A time class with fewer than 2,000 training rows gets no table; the app then uses `all`.
- The app (`rangeFromTable`) rounds the low end down and the high end up to a multiple of 50 and
  widens the range to at least 100 points.

## How much one game's accuracy says about rating

Measured on all kept rows. "Correlation" is the Pearson correlation between a player's accuracy
in one game and that player's rating; "explained" is its square, the share of the differences in
rating that accuracy accounts for. "Everyone" is the 20th..80th percentile of rating over all
rows of that time class, with accuracy ignored: the range one would give knowing nothing about
the game. Compare its width with the median width above.

| time class | rows | correlation | explained | everyone, p20-p80 | width |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 40000 | 0.23 | 5.5 % | 1204-1963 | 759 |
| bullet | 8556 | 0.13 | 1.7 % | 1253-2044 | 791 |
| blitz | 18260 | 0.26 | 7.0 % | 1244-2016 | 772 |
| rapid | 12522 | 0.29 | 8.6 % | 1128-1833 | 705 |
| classical | 662 | 0.34 | 11.6 % | 1346-1836 | 490 |

The link is weak: accuracy in a single game moves the range only a little. The estimate is a
rough indication and the wide ranges are what the data supports.

## Caveats

- These are Lichess ratings. Lichess and Chess.com ratings are on different scales and differ by
  rating level and time class; the estimate is not a Chess.com rating.
- Accuracy here comes from Lichess's server evaluations (fishnet, Stockfish, depth and settings
  chosen by Lichess). The app evaluates with its own engine at its own depth, so its accuracies,
  and therefore the ranges, can differ somewhat for the same game.
- Games with server analysis are not a random sample of all games: they are games someone asked
  to have analysed.
- One month of games, from the start of that month's file.
- The range covers the middle 60 % of players at a given accuracy in one game. One game says
  little about a player; the ranges are wide on purpose.
